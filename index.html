Common questions

How do you vet each apartment?

Every apartment clears five checks: a verified track record, a full video walkthrough, a real conversation with the host, reliability basics (WiFi, water, security), and ongoing guest feedback. Stays that fall below standard are removed.

What is the Trove Promise?

If anything on our vetting checklist fails during your stay, we find you another vetted stay in the Collection, or credit the affected nights. Read the full promise →

Is booking through Karibu Trove free?

Yes. Browsing and enquiring costs nothing. If you book direct through us you typically save around 10% compared to the Airbnb rate — no account or membership needed.

Can I book via WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is our primary booking channel. Tap "Ask about this stay" on any listing and it opens a pre-filled message to our team. We reply within a few hours.
